Our Story

From a single roaster
to a daily ritual.

D'Urban began as a 1kg sample roaster in a back-of-shop kitchen in 2014 — a small obsession with what coffee could taste like when nothing was rushed. Ten years on, we still source direct from a handful of farms we know by name, and roast every Tuesday morning before the doors open.

The room is quiet on purpose. The cups are heavy on purpose. Everything else, we leave to the beans.

— Mara & Theo D'UrbanFounders

The Process

Every cup begins
twelve seconds
before it's poured.

01
Grind, weighed to the gram
18.5g of single-origin, ground moments before extraction.
02
Tamp, level & firm
A consistent puck is the difference between a cup and a great cup.
03
Extract — 27 seconds
Pressure, temperature, and time. Nothing more, nothing less.
04
Served, in your hands
Honey-thick crema, ribbon of tiger-stripe. Drink it warm.
